   ST4-15  https://www.centuryspring.com/shop/st4-15cs
     standard-units table as returned: Material "302/304 Stainless Steel"; Outside Diameter "0.25 in";
     Inside Diameter "0.22 in"; Wire Diameter "0.015 in"; Length "20 in"; Pitch "0.125 in"; Rate "0.6 lbs-in";
     Rate Constant "0.05 lbf"; Solid Length Constant "0.13 in"; Suggested Max Load "6.4 lbf"
     Reading [ARITH]: rate x length = 0.6 lbf = 2.669 N, so a cut length L0 has R = 2.669 N / L0 ("Rate Constant
     0.05 lbf" is the same 0.6 per foot, 0.6 / 12). Solid length 0.13 x L0. Cross-check from the page's own
     wire, pitch and OD [ARITH, G = 69 GPa for 302 stainless EST, not on the page]: d 0.381 mm, D 5.97 mm,
     8 coils per inch, R(1 in) = G d^4 / (8 D^3 n) = 0.61 lbs/in, matching 0.6. The "6.4 lbf" suggested max load is
     out of line with the series (ST3-15 0.8, ST4-17 0.9, ST4-20 1.4 lbf) and is not relied on; the shear stress at
     2.1 N is 8 F D / (pi d^3) x Wahl 1.09 = 0.63 GPa [ARITH], under the 0.45 x tensile static limit for 0.015 in
     302 wire (about 1 GPa [EST: ASTM A313 tensile not read this session]).
     Cut to L0 = 88.9 mm (3.5 in): R = 2.669 / 88.9 = 0.03002 N/mm; solid 0.13 x 88.9 = 11.56 mm.
