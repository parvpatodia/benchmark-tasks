 General 10" Contour Gage - 833 The #833 10 Inch Contour Gage can duplicate any shape. Applications include pattern and mold making, and fitting carpet, tiles or linoleum against and around door casings and pipes.  The body can create profiles up to 10 in. long 3 in. deep fingers can measure profiles up to 1-1/4 in. long Made of tough, durable plastic Graduations: Every 8th of an inch and quarter centimeter  Length: 10" Contour gages can duplicate any shape. This tool can be used for pattern and mold making, duplicating parts and transferring prototype contours. Inch graduation on body.
