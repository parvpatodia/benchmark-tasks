Excerpt (sentences containing 'magnet', verbatim):
- iPhone, MagSafe accessories, and Apple Pencil contain magnets as well as components and/or radios that emit electromagnetic fields.
- These magnets and electromagnetic fields might interfere with medical devices.
- Manufacturers often provide recommendations on the safe use of their devices around wireless or magnetic products to prevent possible interference.
- Medical devices such as implanted pacemakers and defibrillators may contain sensors that respond to magnets and radios when in close contact.
